📊 1. Executive Summary & Macro Stance

The current market environment is defined by a "higher-for-longer" interest rate regime coupled with a bifurcated earnings landscape. Institutional capital is rotating away from speculative growth toward companies demonstrating "Fortress Balance Sheets"—those with high free cash flow (FCF) conversion and low net-debt-to-EBITDA ratios. Our quantitative models suggest that while the broader indices remain extended, specific pockets of value exist in the industrial and financial sectors, while momentum remains concentrated in AI-infrastructure and secular software leaders.

**Macro Outlook:** We maintain a neutral-to-bullish stance on U.S. Equities, favoring quality factors over pure beta. Investors should prioritize companies with pricing power to offset persistent sticky inflation.

📐 2. Quantitative Screening Methodology & Financial Mathematics

Our proprietary screening engine aggregates data across four distinct investment philosophies:

1. Graham (Value): Focuses on Net-Net working capital and P/E < 15x.

2. Buffett (Quality): Prioritizes ROE > 15% and consistent 10-year EPS growth.

3. Lynch (GARP): Targets PEG ratios < 1.0 and earnings acceleration.

4. O’Neil (CANSLIM): Emphasizes relative strength (RS) > 80 and volume-backed breakouts.

**Data Integrity:** All metrics are normalized against the S&P 500 baseline. We utilize a 200-day Simple Moving Average (SMA) filter to ensure we are not catching "falling knives" in the value segments.
