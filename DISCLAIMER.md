# ⚖️ Monolith Intelligence — Legal & Regulatory Compliance Notice

## 1. SEC Publisher's Exemption

Monolith Intelligence (`monolithintelligence.com`) is a general interest, broad-market financial news publication and quantitative market research service. Monolith Intelligence operates under the **Publisher's Exemption** set forth in Section 202(a)(11)(D) of the U.S. Investment Advisers Act of 1940 (15 U.S.C. § 80b-2(a)(11)(D)).

- **Non-Personalized Content**: All research reports, stock screening cards, performance tracking metrics, and commentary are general, impersonal, and addressed to the broad public.
- **No Individualized Advice**: We do not evaluate, account for, or adapt recommendations to any individual investor's financial situation, risk tolerance, tax status, or investment objectives.
- **No Fiduciary Relationship**: Accessing this publication, subscribing to our email newsletter, or viewing our public GitHub track record repository does not establish an advisory, fiduciary, or client relationship.

## 2. Investment Risk Warning

- **Risk of Loss**: Equity market investments involve substantial risk of loss, including potential loss of principal. Past performance is not indicative or a guarantee of future returns.
- **Independent Due Diligence**: Readers must perform their own independent financial analysis, conduct due diligence, and consult with a licensed investment advisor or certified financial planner (CFP) before executing any stock transactions.

## 3. Data Integrity & Live Audits

- Market data and closing stock prices are sourced from third-party exchanges via Yahoo Finance (`yfinance`). While we strive for absolute accuracy in our continuous performance audits, we do not guarantee the completeness or timeliness of external price feeds.
- Hypothetical or tracked paper performance metrics do not reflect transaction costs, broker commissions, slippage, or tax consequences.
