# Investment Allocation Report: Strategic Equity Portfolio

**Date:** June 24, 2026

**Objective:** Balanced Wealth Creation (Income + Growth)  
**Time Horizon:** 10 Years (Re-evaluated Annually)

---

### 1. Investor Profile Summary
You are a **Moderate Risk** investor with a long-term horizon of 10 years. Your objective is a dual-engine approach: generating consistent passive income through high-dividend yielders while capturing capital appreciation through fundamentally sound, undervalued growth stocks.

### 2. Asset Allocation Strategy
To balance your portfolio, we will utilize a **60/40 split** between **Value/Dividend** (Stability) and **Growth/Momentum** (Appreciation). 

*   **Value/Dividend (60%):** Focus on high-yield, low P/E energy and financial stocks to provide a "floor" for your portfolio.
*   **Growth/Momentum (40%):** Focus on companies with high ROE and strong technical positioning to drive alpha.

*Note: This allocation is valid for the next 12 months. We will conduct a formal portfolio review in June 2027 to adjust for market cycles and corporate performance.*
